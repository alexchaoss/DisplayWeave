- **App rules redesign:** choose the app, its region and how DisplayWeave handles it, then Save rule. Each option has a short summary, a tooltip and an explanation; saved rules are listed with their app, region and option.
- **Borderless in region:** removes a windowed game's title bar and border from outside and sizes it to the region; nothing is loaded into the app. Pausing, releasing, removing the rule or exiting puts the title bar and the original place back, and updates wait while a window is shown this way.
- **Centered fullscreen:** a guided, read-only option. App rules shows the resolution for the middle region, whether Windows offers it, and the graphics driver steps; the rule leaves the game's window alone.
- A display size change pauses handling and resumes it by itself once the display is back to its layout size, without rebuilding the layout.
- A borderless fullscreen window that resizes itself back is left alone after one move, with advice to use Borderless in region.
