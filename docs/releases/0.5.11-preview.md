- **Automatic handling never loads anything into an app.** It fits maximized and borderless fullscreen windows into their region from outside. Always-on-top, click-through and never-activated windows, such as overlays and capture tools, are left alone. Exclusive fullscreen is never confined.
- The component loads only for Force fullscreen rules and their shortcuts; its button warns that anti-cheat software can treat it as tampering.
- **Updates no longer wait for fitted windows.** DisplayWeave leaves them in their regions when it exits for an update, and the next version takes them over where they are.
