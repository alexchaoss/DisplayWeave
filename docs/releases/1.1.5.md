- **Every window of an app is kept in its region.** When an app had several windows open at start (for example two windows restored at sign-in) and a Force fullscreen rule or the component was handling one of them, the others were skipped and filled the whole display. They are now fitted into their region like any other window, and the first window is left as it was.

**Known issues**

- An app that maximizes itself while a Force fullscreen rule confines it is left un-maximized at its region's size when handling pauses. Maximize it again.
- Restart as administrator still starts the app from your user-writable install folder; see SECURITY.md, "Known limits".
