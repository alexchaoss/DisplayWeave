- **Browser and browser-based app windows never get the component;** they are fitted from outside only. A browser could crash with the component loaded.
- **File Explorer folder windows** maximize into regions, fitted from outside. Nothing is ever loaded into Windows' shell, and the desktop and taskbar keep their full size.
- A maximized window is fitted the moment it maximizes instead of at the next check, so the full-width flash is much shorter.
- The version shows in the window title, and DisplayWeave has a new icon.

Update from 0.5.6-preview: its automatic handling can load the component into browser windows.
